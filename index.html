METHODOLOGY

Fixed national scoring rules

The national index uses fixed ranges. Weak or buyer-friendly conditions receive a low “greed” score; strong, competitive conditions receive a high score. Regional beta scores use the same component rules but renormalise only across fields with verified data and always show coverage.
